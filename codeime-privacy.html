Privacy Policy

CodeIME Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

CodeIME is an iOS developer keyboard and an SSH workspace for your own servers. The keyboard works entirely on your device. SSH Dev connects only to the servers you set up, and what you do there stays between your device and those servers.

CodeIME does not collect personal data, does not track users, has no accounts or analytics, and the keyboard does not require Full Access.

Data Collection

The developer of CodeIME does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The keyboard extension does not send keystrokes, commands, prediction text, typed content, or usage data to any server.

The CodeIME app talks to the network only for the things described below: connecting to your servers, delivering notifications you turn on, and App Store purchases.

Data Stored On Your Device

To rank its predictions, the keyboard keeps a history of the commands you run in terminals and code editors. This history is saved only inside the keyboard on your device. It is never sent anywhere and is not shared with the CodeIME app.

Lines that look like passwords, tokens, or keys are not saved, and neither are lines that start with a space. Chat and note fields that use autocorrection are never recorded.

You can pin, forget, or clear saved commands by holding a prediction. Deleting CodeIME removes the history.

SSH Dev keeps your imported ssh_config, your host settings and saved commands, known host keys, and recent agent task conversations on your device. SSH keys created in the app are generated on the device and kept in the iOS Keychain or the Secure Enclave. Passwords you type to sign in or to install a key are used for that sign-in and are not saved.

Connections to Your Servers

SSH Dev connects directly from your device to the servers in your configuration, through the jump hosts you configure, or through Cloudflare Access when a server uses it. Terminal sessions, port forwarding, and agent tasks travel only between your device and your servers. They do not pass through servers run by the developer of CodeIME.

When you set up Agent Tasks on a host, CodeIME installs a small helper in ~/.codeime on that server. It reads the Claude Code and Codex sessions there and sends them to the app over your SSH connection.

Cloudflare Access sign-in happens in a web view with your organization's Cloudflare Access, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The resulting sign-in token is kept in the Keychain on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on task notifications, your device's push token is given to the helper on the servers you choose and to the CodeIME push relay, which forwards notifications to the Apple Push Notification service. The relay does not store push tokens or notification content.

A notification from your server carries only random identifiers and the kind of event. Host names and task titles are filled in on your device, so neither the relay nor Apple sees them. Turning notifications off removes your device from those servers.

Purchases

SSH Dev subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. CodeIME does not receive your payment information; whether SSH Dev is unlocked is checked on your device.

Keyboard Full Access

CodeIME does not require keyboard Full Access. The keyboard is designed to provide local command predictions and developer shortcuts without network permission.

Command Predictions

CodeIME predictions are generated on device from the current command fragment, built-in command patterns, and the on-device command history described above. In the CodeIME app's own terminal, paths and branch names can also be completed from your server over your SSH connection.

Network Access

CodeIME does not use network services for analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, synchronization, or server-side prediction.

Tracking

CodeIME does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Third-Party Services

CodeIME does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs. It uses Apple's services for purchases and push notifications, and the push relay runs on Cloudflare Workers.

Children's Privacy

CodeIME does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
