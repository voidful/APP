DailyWardrobe 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

摘要

  • 我們不收集任何資料。DailyWardrobe 沒有帳號、廣告、分析工具、追蹤或我們的伺服器。
  • 照片與衣櫥預設只留在你的 iPhone。衣物辨識、去背、命名與搭配建議都在手機上完成。
  • 只有在你明確選用「用自己的電腦解析」並逐張確認時，照片才會傳到你自己指定的電腦。
  • App 不讀取你的位置或天氣，也不評論身材。

儲存在你 iPhone 上的資料

衣物名稱、類別、顏色、標籤、穿搭紀錄，以及你收進衣櫥的去背圖與穿搭照片，都存在 App 自己的資料夾，使用 iOS 檔案保護，並排除在 iCloud 與電腦的系統備份之外。刪除 App 會一併刪除這些資料。

你可以用「匯出整個衣櫥」把資料存成一個 .dailywardrobe 檔，存放位置由你選擇。這個檔案沒有加密，含有你的照片；若存到雲端資料夾，會依該服務的條款同步。

主畫面小工具只讀取 App 放在 App Group 共用資料夾中的小型摘要：這週記錄的日期，以及幾張衣物縮圖。全身穿搭照片不會放進小工具。

權限

  • 相機：只在你按下拍照時使用，用來拍全身穿搭照。
  • 照片：使用系統的照片挑選器，App 只會拿到你選的那一張，不會讀取整個圖庫。
  • 通知（選用）：每日「今天穿了什麼？」與換季提醒都在本機排程，不經過任何伺服器。
  • 區域網路（選用）：只在你連自己的電腦解析時使用。

用自己的電腦解析（選用）

這個功能預設關閉。啟用後，App 只接受你輸入的 HTTPS 位址，且必須在這部裝置或你的私人網路上；每張照片送出前都會顯示目的地並請你確認。照片只傳到那台電腦，我們不會收到。你也可以完全離線，以匯出與匯入檔案的方式交換結果。

靈感文章

靈感頁的文章摘要已內建在 App 中。點選後會用 Safari 開啟原始網站，那些網站有各自的隱私權政策；App 不會附上你的照片或任何衣櫥資料。

第三方

App 沒有使用第三方分析、廣告或追蹤服務。衣物分割使用 Meta 的 SAM 2.1 Tiny 模型（Apache-2.0），模型隨 App 安裝並在手機上執行，不會連網。Apple 可能依其政策提供 App Store 的下載與當機統計，這些資料由 Apple 處理，我們只會看到 Apple 提供的彙總數據。

兒童

App 不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不收集任何人的個人資料。

政策更新

若 App 的資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期。

聯絡我們

對隱私有任何問題，請寄信至 support@eric-lam.com。

DailyWardrobe Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Summary

  • We collect no data. DailyWardrobe has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or servers of ours.
  • Photos and your wardrobe stay on your iPhone by default. Finding clothes, cutting them out, naming them and suggesting outfits all happen on the phone.
  • A photo leaves your iPhone only if you explicitly choose "Analyze on your own computer" and confirm each photo, and then it goes only to the computer you entered.
  • The app never reads your location or weather, and never comments on your body.

Data stored on your iPhone

Garment names, categories, colors, tags, outfit records, and the cutouts and outfit photos you keep are stored in the app's own folder with iOS file protection, and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

"Export whole wardrobe" saves everything as one .dailywardrobe file, wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted and contains your photos; a cloud folder will sync it under that service's terms.

The Home Screen widget reads only a small summary the app places in its shared App Group folder: the days you logged this week and a few garment thumbnails. Full-body outfit photos are never given to the widget.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap to take a full-body outfit photo.
  • Photos: the system photo picker gives the app only the photo you choose; it never reads your whole library.
  • Notifications (optional): the daily "What did you wear today?" and season reminders are scheduled on the device, never through a server.
  • Local network (optional): used only when you connect your own computer for analysis.

Analyze on your own computer (optional)

This is off by default. When used, the app accepts only an HTTPS address you enter on this device or your private network, and shows the destination and asks before sending each photo. The photo goes only to that computer; we never receive it. You can also exchange files completely offline by exporting and importing.

Inspiration articles

Article summaries are built into the app. Tapping one opens the original website in Safari, which has its own privacy policy; the app never attaches your photos or wardrobe data.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. Garments are segmented with Meta's SAM 2.1 Tiny model (Apache-2.0), installed with the app and run on the phone without a network connection. Apple may provide App Store download and crash statistics under its own policies; Apple processes that data, and we only see the aggregate figures Apple provides.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@eric-lam.com.
