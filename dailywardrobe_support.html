DailyWardrobe 支援

每天一張，認識你的衣櫥

有問題、建議或發現錯誤，請寄信到 support@eric-lam.com，並附上 iPhone 型號與 iOS 版本。請不要寄送含有臉部或其他個人資訊的照片，除非你願意分享。

怎麼把衣服加進衣櫥？

在「今天」拍一張全身照或從相簿選一張。App 會在 iPhone 上找出上身、下身與鞋子並去背，帶入建議的名稱和顏色。確認無誤後按「收好」，每一件都要你確認才會加入。

有衣服沒被找到，或去背不完整

自動辨識目前只分辨上身、下身與鞋子，外套、包包與配件可能被合併或漏掉。你可以在照片上拖曳框出漏掉的衣物，或點開單品用修邊筆刷擦掉多餘部分、補回漏掉的地方；補回時只會使用原照片的像素。光線充足、全身入鏡、背景單純時效果最好。

換手機或想備份

到「設定」的「備份與換手機」選「匯出整個衣櫥」，把檔案存到「檔案」App、電腦或隨身碟。新手機安裝後選「從備份還原」並挑選這個檔案。衣櫥資料不會自動進入 iCloud 或電腦備份，請自己保留一份。

提醒與小工具

每日「今天穿了什麼？」與換季提醒在「設定」中開啟，已記錄的日子不會提醒。主畫面小工具可在主畫面長按後加入，顯示這週的穿搭與一件好久沒穿的衣服。

換季收納

依你設定的厚薄，一次收起或拿回冬衣、夏衣。換季時間依 iPhone 地區設定判斷南北半球，熱帶地區不提示換季。

用自己的電腦解析

這是選用功能，需要你在自己的電腦上執行 HTTPS 服務。每張照片送出前都會請你確認目的地。不使用時，App 完全不需要網路。

資料與隱私

照片與衣櫥預設只留在這部 iPhone，沒有帳號、廣告或追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。

DailyWardrobe Support

One photo a day, get to know your wardrobe

For questions, suggestions or bugs, email support@eric-lam.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't send photos that show your face or other personal details unless you're happy to share them.

How do I add clothes?

On "Today", take a full-body photo or choose one from your library. The app finds the top, bottoms and shoes on your iPhone, cuts them out and fills in a suggested name and color. When they look right, tap to put them away; nothing is added until you confirm each piece.

A piece was missed or cut out poorly

Automatic recognition currently tells tops, bottoms and shoes apart, so jackets, bags and accessories may be merged or missed. Drag a box on the photo around anything missed, or open a piece and use the touch-up brush to erase extras or restore missing parts; restored areas only use pixels from your original photo. Good light, your whole body in frame and a plain background work best.

New phone or backup

In Settings, under "Backup and new phone", choose "Export whole wardrobe" and save the file to the Files app, a computer or a USB drive. On the new phone, choose "Restore from backup" and pick that file. Wardrobe data is not included in iCloud or computer backups, so keep your own copy.

Reminders and widget

Turn on the daily "What did you wear today?" and season reminders in Settings; days you've already logged are skipped. Add the widget by touching and holding the Home Screen; it shows this week's outfits and a piece you haven't worn in a while.

Seasonal storage

Puts winter or summer clothes away, or brings them back, all at once based on the warmth you set. Season timing follows the hemisphere of your iPhone's region setting; there are no season prompts in the tropics.

Analyze on your own computer

This optional feature needs an HTTPS service you run on your own computer. The app asks you to confirm the destination before sending each photo. Without it, the app needs no network at all.

Data and privacy

Photos and your wardrobe stay on this iPhone by default, with no account, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
