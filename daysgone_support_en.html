Daysgone Support

Find photos of the same person on your iPhone or iPad and move them to the Hidden album in Photos after you review each one. All recognition happens on your device.

Contact

For questions, suggestions, or bug reports, email support@eric-lam.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and app version (shown in About & Privacy in the app).

How to use it

  • Photo access: Allow Read and Write access to Photos. You can choose all or some photos.
  • Reference faces: Choose 2–6 clear photos of the same person from different angles. In group photos, tap the face you mean.
  • Scan: The app compares each photo on your device. You can switch apps and the scan keeps going in the background; it pauses when the screen locks and resumes when you come back.
  • Review & Hide: The closest matches come first. New matches start selected; tap “Not them” for anyone else. Photos move to Hidden only after you confirm the list.

FAQ

Are photos deleted?

No. Daysgone only moves photos to the Hidden album in Photos. It never deletes any photo.

How do I unhide photos?

Open Photos → Albums → Hidden (you may need Face ID to unlock it) → select photos → Unhide. iOS locks the Hidden album, so Daysgone can't restore photos for you with one tap. See Apple's guide.

Why weren't some photos found?

Similarity isn't proof of identity. Small, turned, blocked, or much older faces may be missed; a few clearer reference photos from different angles usually help. iCloud photos that aren't downloaded to the device aren't analyzed—download them in Photos first, then run an incremental scan.

Why am I asked to scan again?

When photo access or the set of accessible photos changes, the app asks you to confirm again so it never hides photos outside your choice. Rescanning only analyzes new or changed photos.

What about iCloud Photos?

With iCloud Photos on, hidden status syncs to your other devices. If your accessible photos include an iCloud Shared Library, other members won't see the hidden photos either.

Are videos supported?

Daysgone analyzes still photos and the cover image of Live Photos. Videos aren't supported.
