HaptiEDC

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Publisher: voidful · Contact: support@eric-lam.com

In short: HaptiEDC has no accounts, no backend server, no ads, tracking or analytics, and never sends your interactions, ratings or preferences to the developer. The app does not use the network.

Local preferences and achievements

Material, haptics and sound switches, calibration, each toy's feel settings and My Snap are stored in the app's local data. Achievements additionally store action counts, unlock dates and discovered hidden achievements. None of this is uploaded.

Study records

The optional study comparison stores, on device: variants and their random order, five ratings, timing, app and OS version, device model, haptic parameters, playback status, and the grip and case context you choose. Records use a random code; the device name, serial number, advertising identifier or any permanent device identifier are not stored.

Motion sensors

Gravity, acceleration and rotation samples used by the ball tray are used in memory only while you play. Raw motion history is not saved or written to study files. Micro-sounds are synthesized in real time; the app does not use the microphone.

Sharing and system backup

Only when you export study JSON yourself does the app hand the file to the system share sheet; you choose the recipient and location. Exports may include device model, timing and ratings. iOS backups may include preferences and study files; achievement data is excluded from backup by default.

Retention and removal

"Reset settings" restores feedback and toy preferences while keeping My Snap, study records and achievements; achievements can be reset separately after confirmation. Deleting the app removes its data from this device; "Offload App" and system backups may keep data. Exported copies must be deleted wherever you saved them.

Children

HaptiEDC does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example by adding a network service), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before release and change the effective date above.
