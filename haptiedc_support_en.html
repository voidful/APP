HaptiEDC Support

Eight hands-on tactile toys for iPhone: magnetic slider, ratchet coin, clicker switch, worry stone, thumb roller, fidget spinner, spring plunger and ball tray. No account or network needed.

The app interface is in Traditional Chinese. The toys themselves are gesture-driven and need no reading.

Contact

For questions, suggestions or bug reports, email support@eric-lam.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, which toy you were using and what you did.

How to use

  • Collection: tap any toy to play with it. Gestures and buttons are all on one page.
  • Tune the feel: each toy has its own controls for strength, crispness, resistance and detail, plus a material choice.
  • Haptics and sound: the two buttons at the bottom turn vibration and micro-sounds on or off independently.
  • Office mode: turn it on in Settings to lower intensity and mute micro-sounds.
  • Achievements: the trophy at the top right of the collection. Some are hidden: play a different way and you may discover them.

FAQ

Why is there no sound?

HaptiEDC's micro-sounds follow the iPhone Ring/Silent switch, so they stay quiet in silent mode. Also check that the in-app sound button is on, media volume is above zero, and you're not using the Office Silent material or office mode.

Why is there no vibration?

Make sure the in-app haptics button is on and System Haptics is enabled in Settings → Sounds & Haptics. Haptics need an iPhone that supports Core Haptics; cases and grip also change how it feels.

The balls don't roll when I tilt the phone

Check that the gyro button on the ball tray page is on. With the gyro off you can shake the balls by dragging on the tray. With Reduce Motion enabled the tray stays still, and "Shake" shows the result of one collision directly.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Preferences, achievements and study records stay on your device and are only handed to the share sheet if you export them yourself. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this a medical product?

No. HaptiEDC is a tactile toy. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If your hand feels tired, lower the intensity or take a break.
