inpiano Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

Data Collection

inpiano does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data.

Local Data

The app stores trial and unlock state locally on your device using Apple system storage. Imported score files stay on your device and are used only to let you play them inside the app.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. inpiano does not receive or store your payment information.

Advertising And Tracking

inpiano does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Support

If you contact support, any information you choose to provide will be used only to respond to your request.
