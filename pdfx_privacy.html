PDFX

Privacy Policy

PDFX is a local PDF editing utility for macOS. It is designed to work with files you choose on your Mac without accounts, tracking, analytics, or cloud upload.

Effective date: May 9, 2026

Data Collection

PDFX does not collect, sell, share, or track personal data.

PDFX does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, account creation, telemetry, or cloud synchronization.

PDF Documents And Files

PDFX works with files that you explicitly choose through macOS open/save panels, drag and drop, pasteboard actions, or Apple continuity import features.

Your PDF documents, signatures, images, annotations, and edited output remain on your Mac unless you choose to share or upload them using another app or service.

Network Use

PDFX does not require network access for its core PDF editing features.

App Store Privacy

For App Store privacy responses, PDFX should be represented as not collecting data, provided the shipped version remains free of analytics, accounts, telemetry, ads, crash reporting SDKs, and cloud services.
