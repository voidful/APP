PostGuide 構圖教練 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

摘要

  • 我們不收集任何資料。PostGuide 沒有帳號、廣告、分析工具、追蹤，也沒有我們的伺服器。
  • 相機畫面只在你的 iPhone 上處理。找出人、臉與寵物，以及構圖提示，都在手機上完成；畫面不會被儲存或傳出。
  • 拍好的照片只有在你按下「儲存」時才會存進「照片」；App 不讀取你的圖庫。

相機與照片

取景時，App 在手機上分析相機畫面，用白點和圓圈提示手機怎麼移。這些畫面只用於即時提示，不會寫入儲存空間，也不會離開手機。

你按下快門，或開啟「穩定後自動拍照」後由 App 自動拍下的照片，會先留在這次使用的暫存相簿（最多 12 張，只在記憶體中，關閉 App 後清除）。只有你按「儲存」時，才會新增到「照片」。

從相簿挑照片看建議或「照這張拍」時，App 使用系統的照片挑選器，只會拿到你選的那一張，不會讀取整個圖庫，也不會另外保存它。

儲存在你 iPhone 上的資料

  • 設定：照片比例、風格、美顏、格線、構圖範圍與連拍。
  • 「照這張拍」的免費次數：今天用了幾次，隔天重算。
  • 實拍紀錄：每次用相機拍照的日期、鏡頭、人數、是否自動拍照、按快門時是否已到位，以及照片的一句建議；只有文字和數字，不含照片，最多保留最近 500 筆。可以在「拍攝設定 › 進階 › 實拍紀錄」清除。

這些資料存在 App 自己的資料夾，和其他 App 資料一樣會包含在 iPhone 的系統備份中。刪除 App 會一併刪除。

權限與感測器

  • 相機：顯示取景畫面並在手機上提示構圖。
  • 動作感測：讀取手機的水平與前後傾斜，提示把手機拿正；只在手機上使用。
  • 新增照片：只在你按「儲存」時新增照片，不讀取既有相簿。

PostGuide Pro 購買

PostGuide Pro 透過 App Store 一次購買。付款、收據與退款都由 Apple 處理，我們不會收到你的付款資料、Apple 帳號或聯絡方式。App 只在 iPhone 上透過 Apple 的 StoreKit 確認這部裝置是否已購買；「恢復購買」也只是請 Apple 重新同步這項紀錄。

第三方

App 沒有使用第三方分析、廣告或追蹤服務。人、臉與寵物的偵測使用隨 App 安裝的模型與 Apple 的 Vision 框架，在手機上執行，不會連網。Apple 可能依其政策提供 App Store 的下載與當機統計，這些資料由 Apple 處理，我們只會看到 Apple 提供的彙總數據。

兒童

App 不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不收集任何人的個人資料。

政策更新

若 App 的資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期。

聯絡我們

對隱私有任何問題，請寄信至 support@eric-lam.com。

PostGuide Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Summary

  • We collect no data. PostGuide has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or servers of ours.
  • The camera view is processed only on your iPhone. Finding people, faces and pets, and the framing tips, all happen on the phone; the view is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • A photo goes into Photos only when you tap Save; the app never reads your library.

Camera and photos

While you frame a shot, the app analyses the camera view on the phone and shows, with a white dot and a circle, how to move the phone. These frames are used only for the live tips; they are never written to storage and never leave the phone.

Photos you take, or that the app takes for you when the automatic shutter is on, stay in this session's album (up to 12 photos, in memory only, cleared when the app closes). They are added to Photos only when you tap Save.

To get advice on a photo, or to shoot like it, you choose it with the system photo picker: the app receives only that photo, never reads your whole library, and does not keep a copy.

Data stored on your iPhone

  • Settings: photo shape, style, beauty, grid, framing and burst.
  • Free uses of "Shoot like this photo": how many were used today; the count restarts the next day.
  • Shooting log: for each photo taken with the camera, the date, the lens, the number of people, whether the automatic shutter took it, whether the framing was ready at the shutter, and the photo's one-line advice. It holds only text and numbers, never the photo, and keeps the latest 500 entries. Clear it under Settings › Advanced › Shooting log (實拍紀錄).

This data is stored in the app's own folder and, like other app data, is included in your iPhone's system backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions and sensors

  • Camera: shows the viewfinder and gives framing tips on the phone.
  • Motion sensors: read how level the phone is and its forward tilt, to suggest holding it straight; used only on the phone.
  • Add to Photos: adds a photo only when you tap Save; never reads your existing library.

PostGuide Pro purchase

PostGuide Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple handles payment, receipts and refunds; we never receive your payment details, Apple Account or contact information. The app only asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone whether this device has the purchase, and "Restore Purchases" only asks Apple to sync that record again.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. People, faces and pets are found with models installed with the app and with Apple's Vision framework, run on the phone without a network connection. Apple may provide App Store download and crash statistics under its own policies; Apple processes that data, and we only see the aggregate figures Apple provides.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@eric-lam.com.
