PostGuide 構圖教練 支援

白點進到圓圈，就拍得好看

有問題、建議或發現錯誤，請寄信到 support@eric-lam.com，並附上 iPhone 型號與 iOS 版本。請不要寄送含有臉部或其他個人資訊的照片，除非你願意分享。

白點和圓圈是什麼？

圓圈是她的臉該在的位置，白點是她的臉現在的位置。照畫面下方的提示移動手機，例如「手機往左移一點」「往前靠近一點」，每次只說一件事。白點進到圓圈時，圓圈變綠、手機輕震，就可以按快門。不用叫她走位，移動的是手機。

照這張拍

按快門左邊的相簿，選一張喜歡的照片，再按「照這張拍」。橫式、限動、方形或合照都可以。圓圈會擺到和那張照片一樣的位置與大小，照著提示拍就好；拍完可以用「一鍵修好構圖」轉正並裁切，貼近參考照。

合照、自拍與寵物

畫面裡有兩人以上時，每個人都有自己的圓圈；2 到 50 人都可以。用前鏡頭手持時會改成自拍構圖，兩人自拍會先提示頭靠近一點。貓狗也會找到並提示構圖。

自動拍照與連拍

在畫面上點「自動快門」，構圖到位並穩定後會自動拍下。「拍攝設定」裡可以開啟連拍：到位後連續拍幾張，留下沒人閉眼、臉最清楚的那幾張。每次打開 App，自動快門都先關著，避免一開相機就自己拍。

拍完的一句建議

每張照片下方會有一句建議，例如「頭頂被切到：下次手機往上一點」；拍得好就說「構圖到位」。能靠裁切改善的照片會附「一鍵修好構圖」，原圖會一起保留。

美顏與風格

美顏預設「自然」：只淡化斑點和膚色不均，保留膚色與毛孔。照片風格預設「依光線自動」。開著美顏或風格時，原色照片一併保留，可以在檢視時切換比較。

照片存在哪裡？

拍好的照片先放在這次使用的暫存相簿（快門左邊），最多 12 張，關閉 App 後清除。想保留就按「儲存」，照片會存進「照片」App。

相機打不開

如果之前沒有允許相機，請到 iPhone 的「設定 › PostGuide › 相機」打開。若是螢幕使用時間或裝置管理限制了相機，請先在那裡解除。

免費版與 PostGuide Pro

免費下載後，即時構圖教練（單人、合照、自拍、寵物）、拍完的一句建議與自然美顏都能一直使用，「照這張拍」每天可以用 3 次。PostGuide Pro 一次購買、沒有訂閱，加上不限次數的「照這張拍」、一鍵修好構圖、連拍挑最好的一張，以及柔和高光、微暖與美顏加強。

在「拍攝設定」左上角的 ✨ 可以購買或「恢復購買」；用到 Pro 功能時也會出現同一個畫面。換手機或重新安裝後，登入同一個 Apple 帳號再按「恢復購買」即可。付款與退款由 Apple 處理，退款請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

PostGuide Support

Get the white dot into the circle, and the photo looks good

For questions, suggestions or bugs, email support@eric-lam.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't send photos that show faces or other personal details unless you're happy to share them.

What are the white dot and the circle?

The circle is where her face should be; the white dot is where her face is now. Move the phone as the tip below the viewfinder says, such as "move the phone a little left" or "come a little closer", one thing at a time. When the dot is inside the circle, the circle turns green and the phone taps lightly: take the photo. You move the phone; nobody has to be told where to stand. (The app's interface is in Traditional Chinese.)

Shoot like this photo (照這張拍)

Tap the album to the left of the shutter, choose a photo you like, then tap 照這張拍. Landscape, story-shaped, square and group photos all work. The circle takes the same place and size as in that photo; follow the tips to shoot. Afterwards, the one-tap fix can straighten and crop the photo to match the reference.

Groups, selfies and pets

With two or more people in view, everyone gets their own circle, for groups of 2 to 50. Holding the front camera switches to selfie framing, and a two-person selfie first asks you to lean your heads together. Cats and dogs are found and framed too.

Automatic shutter and burst

Tap the automatic shutter on screen and the photo is taken once the framing is ready and steady. Burst, in Settings, takes several photos once ready and keeps the ones where nobody blinks and faces are sharpest. The automatic shutter starts off every time you open the app, so the camera never shoots by itself right away.

One line of advice after the shot

Each photo gets one line of advice, such as "the top of the head is cut off: next time, raise the phone a little", or "well framed" when it is. If cropping can improve a photo, the one-tap fix is offered and the original is kept too.

Beauty and styles

Beauty is "Natural" by default: it only softens spots and uneven tone, keeping skin color and texture. The photo style follows the light by default. With beauty or a style on, the original photo is kept as well, and you can compare them when viewing.

Where are my photos?

New photos go to this session's album (left of the shutter), up to 12, cleared when the app closes. Tap Save to keep one in the Photos app.

The camera won't open

If you didn't allow the camera earlier, turn it on in the iPhone's Settings › PostGuide › Camera. If Screen Time or device management restricts the camera, lift the restriction there first.

Free and PostGuide Pro

The free download keeps the live framing coach (one person, groups, selfies, pets), the advice after each shot and natural beauty, with 照這張拍 three times a day. PostGuide Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It adds unlimited 照這張拍, the one-tap fix, burst that keeps the best photo, and the Soft and Warm styles with stronger beauty.

Buy it or tap "Restore Purchases" from the ✨ button at the top left of Settings; the same screen appears when you use a Pro feature. On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple handles payment and refunds; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data and privacy

The camera view is processed only on this iPhone, with no account, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
