Quick Help

Restore Pro

Open SingSpark, go to Help & Privacy, then tap Restore Pro Purchase. Make sure you are using the Apple Account that originally bought the unlock.

Bluetooth Latency

Bluetooth earphones can add delay. Use the latency calibration game once for each headset; SingSpark remembers the route and applies the saved offset.

External Music

SingSpark can mix with music apps playing in the background. It does not read Spotify or Apple Music audio data; pitch analysis uses microphone input only.

Recordings

Recordings are local to your device. They leave the device only if you choose to export or share them through iOS sharing features.
