慢慢懂你 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

摘要

  • 我們不收集任何資料。慢慢懂你沒有帳號、廣告、分析工具、追蹤或伺服器，也不會連上網路。
  • 你們的回答都用說的，App 不提供輸入欄位，也不會錄音。
  • App 不在你的裝置上儲存任何資料。

App 會處理哪些資訊

「包含 18+ 話題」的選擇、目前抽到的卡，以及收尾卡上「今晚翻開過哪些卡」，只存在 App 執行時的記憶體中。關閉 App 後全部消失，下次開啟時 18+ 選項會回到關閉。

App 切到背景時，畫面會以牌背遮住，App 切換器不會顯示題目內容。

權限

慢慢懂你不會要求相機、麥克風、照片、位置、通知、聯絡人或「健康」等任何權限。

第三方

App 沒有使用任何第三方套件或服務。Apple 可能依其政策提供 App Store 的下載與當機統計，這些資料由 Apple 處理，我們只會看到 Apple 提供的彙總數據。

兒童

慢慢懂你為成年情侶設計，不以 18 歲以下使用者為對象。

政策更新

若 App 的資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期。

聯絡我們

對隱私有任何問題，請寄信至 support@eric-lam.com。

Knowing You, Slowly Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Summary

  • We collect no data. Knowing You, Slowly has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or servers, and it never connects to the internet.
  • You answer out loud. The app has no text fields and never records audio.
  • The app stores nothing on your device.

What the app handles

Your "Include 18+ topics" choice, the card you are on, and the list of cards revealed tonight (shown on the closing card) exist only in memory while the app runs. They disappear when the app is closed, and the 18+ option starts off again next time.

When the app moves to the background, a card back covers the screen, so the app switcher never shows a question.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, location, notifications, contacts, or Health.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party code or services. Apple may provide App Store download and crash statistics under its own policies; Apple processes that data, and we only see the aggregate figures Apple provides.

Children

Knowing You, Slowly is designed for adult couples and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@eric-lam.com.
