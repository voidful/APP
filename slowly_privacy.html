慢慢懂你 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

摘要

  • 我們不收集任何資料。慢慢懂你沒有帳號、廣告、分析工具、追蹤，也沒有我們自己的伺服器。只有在你們使用「用 FaceTime 一起翻牌」時，App 才會透過 Apple 的 SharePlay，把牌桌狀態傳給通話中的另一支手機。
  • 你們的回答都用說的，App 不提供輸入欄位，也不會錄音。
  • App 只在你的裝置上記下「翻過哪張卡、在哪一天」，用來畫出你們的星圖；不記下你們說了什麼，隨時可以清除。

存在你裝置上的資料

星圖：每翻開一張卡，App 會在裝置上記下那張卡的編號與日期（例如「第 19 張，2026-10-22」），用來點亮星圖，並讓下次優先抽還沒聊過的卡。這筆紀錄不包含任何回答或對話內容，不會離開你的裝置。你可以在「我們的星圖」頁按「清除星圖」刪除，刪除 App 也會一併移除。

只在執行時使用的資訊

「也聊親密與界線（18+）」的選擇與目前抽到的卡，只存在 App 執行時的記憶體中。關閉 App 後就消失，下次開啟時 18+ 選項會回到關閉。

App 切到背景時，畫面會以牌背遮住，App 切換器不會顯示題目內容。

用 FaceTime 一起翻牌（SharePlay）

在 FaceTime 通話中一起翻牌時，App 會用 Apple 的 SharePlay 把以下牌桌狀態傳給通話中的另一支手機：這一輪的卡片順序（卡片編號）、目前翻到第幾張、是否已翻開、「輕一點」或「深一點」、現在由誰說，以及你們按下的互動道具（例如「我聽見了」「一起呼吸」）。

這些訊息經由 Apple 的 FaceTime 服務以端對端加密傳送，不經過我們的伺服器，我們也無法看到。訊息不包含你們說的話、影像或聲音；通話本身由 FaceTime 處理，App 不會存取相機或麥克風。結束 SharePlay 後，App 不保留這些牌桌狀態；另一支手機只會在它自己的星圖記下翻過的卡片編號與日期。

FaceTime 與 SharePlay 本身的資料處理，依 Apple 的隱私權政策辦理。

權限

慢慢懂你不會要求相機、麥克風、照片、位置、通知、聯絡人或「健康」等任何權限。

第三方

App 沒有使用任何第三方套件或服務。Apple 可能依其政策提供 App Store 的下載與當機統計，這些資料由 Apple 處理，我們只會看到 Apple 提供的彙總數據。

兒童

慢慢懂你為成年情侶設計，不以 18 歲以下使用者為對象。

政策更新

若 App 的資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期。

Knowing You, Slowly Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Summary

  • We collect no data. Knowing You, Slowly has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or servers of our own. Only when you use "Draw together on FaceTime" does the app send the state of the table to the other phone on the call, through Apple's SharePlay.
  • You answer out loud. The app has no text fields and never records audio.
  • On your device, the app records only which card was turned over and on which day, to draw your star map. It never records what you said, and you can clear it at any time.

Data stored on your device

Star map: each time you turn over a card, the app saves that card's number and the date on your device (for example "card 19, 2026-10-22"). It uses this to light your star map and to start the next session with cards you haven't talked about. The record contains no answers or conversation, and it never leaves your device. Delete it with "Clear star map" on the Our star map page; deleting the app removes it too.

Information used only while the app runs

Your "Include intimacy and boundaries (18+)" choice and the card you are on exist only in memory. They disappear when the app is closed, and the 18+ option starts off again next time.

When the app moves to the background, a card back covers the screen, so the app switcher never shows a question.

Drawing together on FaceTime (SharePlay)

When you draw together during a FaceTime call, the app uses Apple's SharePlay to send this table state to the other phone on the call: the order of cards for this round (card numbers), which card you are on, whether it is turned over, "Lighter" or "Deeper", whose turn it is to talk, and the conversation tools you tap (such as "I hear you" or "Breathe together").

These messages travel end-to-end encrypted through Apple's FaceTime service. They never pass through a server of ours, and we cannot see them. They contain nothing you say, and no video or audio; FaceTime handles the call itself, and the app never accesses the camera or microphone. The app keeps none of this table state after SharePlay ends; the other phone only records the card numbers and dates in its own star map.

Apple's privacy policy covers how FaceTime and SharePlay themselves handle data.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, location, notifications, contacts, or Health.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party code or services. Apple may provide App Store download and crash statistics under its own policies; Apple processes that data, and we only see the aggregate figures Apple provides.

Children

Knowing You, Slowly is designed for adult couples and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@eric-lam.com.
