慢慢懂你 支援

一副給兩個人的對話牌。一支手機放在兩人中間，洗牌、翻開一張，輪流用說的回答。

寄信給 support@eric-lam.com

怎麼玩

  1. 在首頁決定要不要「包含 18+ 話題」，再按「開始抽卡」。
  2. 洗牌後，輕觸卡片或按「翻開這張」。第一張翻開前，先對看三秒，先笑出來的人先說。
  3. 覺得太重就按「輕一點」，想多聊就按「深一點」。
  4. 按「換一張」或左右滑開卡片，抽下一張。
  5. 聊夠了，按右上角「一起收尾」。

常見問題

為什麼一開始都是輕鬆的題目？牌堆會先暖身：前兩張最輕，第三到五張最多到中等深度，之後才全部開放。18+ 卡最早從第三張開始出現。

為什麼每次打開，18+ 都是關閉的？App 不儲存任何資料，所以每次都由你們重新選擇。

回答會被記錄嗎？不會。回答都用說的，App 不會錄音，也沒有輸入欄位，更不會連上網路。

可以跳過嗎？任何一張都可以直接換掉，不用說明理由。願意談，不代表願意做。

這是諮商嗎？不是。題目參考了關係研究，但慢慢懂你是對話工具，不是諮商、診斷或危機處理服務。如果對話讓你感到不安全，請離開並尋求信任的人或專業協助。

回報問題

請寄信到 support@eric-lam.com，並附上裝置型號、iOS 版本與遇到的狀況。翻譯有誤也歡迎告訴我們；目前的多語翻譯是 AI 初稿。

Knowing You, Slowly Support

A conversation deck for two. Put one phone between you, shuffle, turn over a card, and take turns answering out loud.

Email support@eric-lam.com

How to play

  1. On the home screen, choose whether to include 18+ topics, then tap "Draw a card".
  2. After the shuffle, tap the card or "Reveal this card". Before the first card, look at each other for three seconds; whoever laughs first goes first.
  3. Tap "Lighter" for a gentler version of the question, or "Deeper" for a follow-up.
  4. Tap "Another card" or swipe the card aside for the next one.
  5. When you've talked enough, tap "Close together" at the top.

FAQ

Why are the first questions so light? The deck warms up: the first two cards are the lightest, cards three to five go up to medium depth, and then the whole deck opens. 18+ cards can appear from the third card on.

Why is 18+ off every time I open the app? The app stores nothing, so you choose again each time.

Are our answers recorded? No. You answer out loud; the app never records audio, has no text fields, and never connects to the internet.

Can we skip a card? Any card can be swapped without a reason. Being willing to talk about something is not agreeing to do it.

Is this therapy? No. The questions draw on relationship research, but the app is a conversation tool, not counseling, diagnosis, or crisis support. If a conversation makes you feel unsafe, step away and reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
