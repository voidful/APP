慢慢懂你 支援

一副給兩個人的對話牌。一支手機放在兩人中間，洗牌、翻開一張，輪流用說的回答。

寄信給 support@eric-lam.com

怎麼玩

  1. 在首頁決定要不要「也聊親密與界線（18+）」，再按「開始抽卡」。
  2. 洗牌後，兩個人各按一次「準備好了」，牌才會翻開。第一張翻開前，先對看三秒，先笑出來的人先說。
  3. 覺得太重就按「輕一點」，想多聊就按「深一點」。卡片右上角的書本圖示會列出這張卡的出處。
  4. 按「換一張」或左右滑開卡片，抽下一張。
  5. 聊夠了，按右上角「一起收尾」。每翻開一張卡，首頁的「我們的星圖」就會點亮一顆星。

常見問題

為什麼一開始都是輕鬆的題目？牌堆會先暖身：前兩張最輕，第三到五張最多到中等深度，之後才全部開放。18+ 卡最早從第三張開始出現。

為什麼每次打開，18+ 都是關閉的？這個選擇不會被儲存，所以每次都由你們重新決定。

星圖記了什麼？只記下翻過哪張卡、在哪一天，不記下任何回答。下次會先抽還沒聊過的卡。想重新開始，可以在「我們的星圖」頁按「清除星圖」。

什麼是留白卡？暖身之後會出現一張編號 0 的留白卡，請你們自己問出一直想問的問題。它只用說的，不會被記錄，也不算進星圖。

回答會被記錄嗎？不會。回答都用說的，App 不會錄音，也沒有輸入欄位。

不在同一個地方，也能一起玩嗎？可以。在 FaceTime 通話中，按首頁的「用 FaceTime 一起翻牌」，兩支手機會看到同一張牌，誰翻開，兩邊就一起翻開。翻牌下方有互動道具：「我來說／換你說」決定現在由誰說；「一起呼吸」讓兩邊一起做兩次深呼吸；「我聽見了」「謝謝你說」「給我一點時間」「想停一下」可以在不打斷對方的情況下傳給對方。是否包含 18+ 卡，由開始一起翻牌的那支手機決定。

卡片下方的「聽的人」是什麼？每張卡都附一個給聽的人的小動作，例如先用一句話說回聽到的、問「然後呢？」，或先不給建議。說的人回答題目，聽的人照著做，輪到下一張再交換。用 FaceTime 一起翻牌時，只有聽的人的手機會顯示。

還有哪些兩個人一起做的動作？同一支手機時，卡片右上角的箭頭可以把卡轉向坐在對面的人。用 FaceTime 一起翻牌時，可以按「擊掌」：兩個人在幾秒內都按下，兩邊會一起出現擊掌成功。

一起翻牌會經過你們的伺服器嗎？不會。牌桌狀態透過 Apple 的 SharePlay 端對端加密，直接傳給通話中的另一支手機；我們沒有伺服器，也看不到。通話與聲音由 FaceTime 處理，App 不會存取麥克風。

可以跳過嗎？任何一張都可以直接換掉，不用說明理由。願意談，不代表願意做。

這是諮商嗎？不是。題目參考了關係研究，但慢慢懂你是對話工具，不是諮商、診斷或危機處理服務。如果對話讓你感到不安全，請離開並尋求信任的人或專業協助。

Knowing You, Slowly Support

A conversation deck for two. Put one phone between you, shuffle, turn over a card, and take turns answering out loud.

Email support@eric-lam.com

How to play

  1. On the home screen, choose whether to include intimacy and boundaries (18+), then tap "Draw a card".
  2. After the shuffle, each of you taps "Ready"; the card turns over once you both have. Before the first card, look at each other for three seconds; whoever laughs first goes first.
  3. Tap "Lighter" for a gentler version of the question, or "Deeper" for a follow-up. The book icon at the top right of a card lists where it comes from.
  4. Tap "Another card" or swipe the card aside for the next one.
  5. When you've talked enough, tap "Close together" at the top. Every card you turn over lights a star in "Our star map" on the home screen.

FAQ

Why are the first questions so light? The deck warms up: the first two cards are the lightest, cards three to five go up to medium depth, and then the whole deck opens. 18+ cards can appear from the third card on.

Why is 18+ off every time I open the app? That choice is never saved, so you decide again each time.

What does the star map keep? Only which card was turned over and on which day, never any answers. Next time the deck starts with cards you haven't talked about. To start over, tap "Clear star map" on the Our star map page.

What is the blank card? After the warm-up, a card numbered 0 asks you to bring the question you’ve been wanting to ask. It is spoken only, never recorded, and not counted in the star map.

Are our answers recorded? No. You answer out loud; the app never records audio and has no text fields.

Can we play when we're apart? Yes. During a FaceTime call, tap "Draw together on FaceTime" on the home screen. Both phones show the same card, and when either of you turns it over, it turns over on both. Below the card are conversation tools: "My turn / Hand it over" shows who is talking; "Breathe together" leads both phones through two slow breaths; "I hear you", "Thank you for sharing", "Give me a moment" and "Can we pause?" reach the other person without interrupting. The phone that starts drawing together decides whether 18+ cards are included.

What is the "Listener" note under the card? Every card comes with one small move for the person listening, such as saying back what they heard in one sentence, asking "And then?", or holding off on advice. One person answers the card, the other does the move, and you swap on the next card. When drawing together on FaceTime, only the listener's phone shows it.

What else do we do together? On one phone, the arrows at the top of a card turn it to face the person sitting opposite. When drawing together on FaceTime, tap "High five": if you both tap within a few seconds, both phones celebrate.

Does drawing together go through your servers? No. Apple's SharePlay sends the table state end-to-end encrypted, straight to the other phone on the call. We have no server and cannot see it. FaceTime handles the call and the sound; the app never accesses the microphone.

Can we skip a card? Any card can be swapped without a reason. Being willing to talk about something is not agreeing to do it.

Is this therapy? No. The questions draw on relationship research, but the app is a conversation tool, not counseling, diagnosis, or crisis support. If a conversation makes you feel unsafe, step away and reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
